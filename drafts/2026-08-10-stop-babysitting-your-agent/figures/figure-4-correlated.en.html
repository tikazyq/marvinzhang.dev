Figure 04 — What the errors look like decides how to review

Fifty different mistakes, or one mistake fifty times

Work alone and errors are independent. Hand one spec to fifty runs and they're the same error. The review must change with them.
One person working alone
Independent and scattered. A sample tells you nothing about the rest.
Only item-by-item review works. Cost grows linearly, and it still misses.
COST ∝ N
One spec run fifty times
Clustered on one offset. Pull ten, see it twice, the other forty likely have it.
Sample for repeated patterns. Two hits and the problem is out in the open.
COST ≪ N
Leverage amplifies bias, not variance. Item-by-item review is designed for the left-hand picture; on the right it's expensive and off-target. The right needs a small sample checked for the same flaw twice.
